# LAUNCH-COPY.md — TrailWatch marketing copy kit (Stage 0.4)

Reusable copy for the distribution runbook (`DISTRIBUTION.md`). Drafts — edit
freely; they're written in the landing's voice (plain, specific, honest, low-
noise, founder-first — no hype, no "revolutionary"). Everything here is truthful
to the product; keep it that way.

**Product facts (source of truth — keep copy consistent with these):**
- TrailWatch watches competitors' **pricing, homepage, and changelog** pages,
  **daily**, filters out trivial edits, AI-summarizes the meaningful ones, and
  sends **one plain-English digest email a week**. Low noise is the whole edge.
- **Free:** $0, no card — 2 competitors, 6 pages, weekly digest, **AI summaries
  included**.
- **Pro:** **$19/mo** or **$190/yr** (2 months free) — 10 competitors, 100 pages,
  daily checks. AI on every plan, even free.
- By **Chandan Dongre**, solo indie founder (House of Ruga).
- Site: `https://trailwatch.houseofruga.com` · Free tool (no signup):
  `https://trailwatch.houseofruga.com/tools/competitor-teardown`

## 5. Indie Hackers post (disclosed founder story)

**Title:** I built a low-noise competitor tracker after drowning in page-change alerts

**Body:**
> I'm a solo founder, and keeping an eye on competitors kept costing me either too
> much money or too much attention.
>
> The enterprise competitive-intelligence tools are built for sales teams —
> battlecards, contracts, a platform to onboard. Overkill for one person. The
> cheaper page-change monitors went the other way: they'd alert me every time a
> competitor fixed a typo or reordered a section. I ended up ignoring both.
>
> So I built TrailWatch. You add a competitor's pricing / homepage / changelog
> pages; it checks them daily, filters out the trivial edits, and emails you one
> plain-English summary a week of what actually changed. The whole design goal was
> *low noise* — a readable email, never a raw diff.
>
> A couple of decisions I'm opinionated about:
> • AI summaries are on every plan, including free (they're the product, not an upsell).
> • Flat pricing, no "contact sales."
>
> If it's useful, there's a free tool that shows the idea in 10 seconds — paste any
> competitor's URL and it gives you a teardown of their positioning and pricing:
> https://trailwatch.houseofruga.com/tools/competitor-teardown
>
> Happy to answer anything about the build (Next.js + Supabase, the noise filter
> was the fiddly part), and I'd love feedback on where the summaries are too terse
> or too chatty.

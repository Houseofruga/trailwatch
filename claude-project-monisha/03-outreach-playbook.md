Condensed from the full Outreach Playbook page Monisha uses day to day. Goal: **25 brands signed up as beta members by January 1, 2027, each with a call booked with Chandan.** The number reported every Friday is **calls booked**.

## The funnel (four doors, one way through)

Doors: **cold email** (fastest, from week 1), **communities** (warm, 3 to 4 weeks), **Reddit** (slow start, account needs age and karma), **X and LinkedIn** (slowest, compounds over months).

1. A conversation starts (5 to 10 a week)
2. They see their competitor in Trailwatch: a finding, a snapshot, or the homepage lookup (4 to 6)
3. They sign up and set up: account, own store, 2+ competitors (2 to 3)
4. **Call booked** with Chandan, after their first Monday briefing (1 to 2)

**What works everywhere:** a specific, true, fresh fact about someone's competitor.
**Where it leaks:** slow replies and sign-ups nobody checks on. Conversations come before new posts every day.

## Cold email, stage by stage

### 1. Find the hook (about 5 minutes)
- Work from the Prospect tracker (198 checked brands). Skincare, body care and supplements first.
- Check the brand's site is live and selling its own products.
- Pick one of the two suggested competitors that's a genuine match (same kind of product, similar price).
- Look the **competitor** up at gettrailwatch.com ("Try it on a competitor") or with the free Sale checker / Store snapshot tools.
- Confirm on the competitor's own site, then note the finding with today's date.

Best hooks, strongest first:
1. A sale with many products or a big discount: "Dewlane has 21 products on sale right now, up to 25% off"
2. Several sell-outs, especially a known product: "9 of Dewlane's 40 products are sold out, including their Barrier Balm"
3. Several launches: "Dewlane launched 6 new products in the last 30 days, including a Night Cream"
4. Both competitors quiet? Skip the brand this week. "Nothing changed" is not a hook.

### 2. Find the person (3 minutes max)
- Founder/co-founder at small brands; head of marketing, growth or e-commerce at bigger ones.
- Use the published contact email (hello@brand.com) and greet the founder by first name. Never guess addresses.
- No name after 3 minutes: "Hi [Brand] team".

### 3. Send, then follow up once
- Plain text from chandan@gettrailwatch.com. No images, attachments or links in the first email.
- Schedule to arrive **8:30 to 10:00 am US Eastern**, Tuesday to Thursday best (7:00 to 8:30 pm IST after November 1).
- **10 new emails a day, never more than 15.** One at a time, no BCC.
- Follow up once, 4 to 5 days later, in the same thread, with a different finding. Then stop.

### 4. Reply within one working day
### 5. Get them signed up and set up (own store + 2 competitors)
### 6. Book the call after their first Monday briefing

## When to change course

- 100 emails with fewer than 2 sign-ups: tell Chandan. Try a new hook type, category or version.
- About 50 emails per version: compare reply rates, keep the best, test a new challenger.
- Many replies, few sign-ups: the reply step leaks. Review 3 recent replies with Chandan.
- The list runs out: ask Chandan for the next 200 (pet, haircare, fragrance, home).
- A community gives nothing after 4 weeks: drop it, try another.
- One channel brings most sign-ups: shift time to it, but keep cold email at 30+ a week until the 25 spots are full.

## Daily routine (India time)

Every day, in order: answer every reply and DM → check new sign-ups → book calls and send prep notes → follow-ups due today → snapshots promised → research and schedule 10 cold emails → X post + 15 minutes of replies → 1 or 2 Reddit answers → update the tracker.

Extra by day:
- **Monday:** write the weekly data post; post it as an X thread; send Chandan LinkedIn drafts.
- **Tuesday:** share the data post in one community; X: one competitor move explained.
- **Wednesday:** 30 minutes of community answers; X: a tip from a guide.
- **Thursday:** snapshot offer in one community (every other week); prep notes for next week's calls; X: building in public.
- **Friday:** channel log; Friday numbers to Chandan; pick next week's category; X: Black Friday countdown.
- **Weekends:** nothing required.

If only 8 hours this week: replies and sign-ups first, then 30 to 40 cold emails, then the data post in two communities, then Reddit and X with whatever's left.

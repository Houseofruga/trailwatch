# Trailwatch: the product

_Facts as of November 7, 2026. If something here conflicts with what Chandan says, Chandan wins._

## In one line

Trailwatch watches your competitors' Shopify stores and tells you when they launch, discount or sell out, with a Monday briefing of what it means for your own products.

Homepage headline: **"Your competitor started a sale on Thursday. You found out on Monday."**

## The problem it solves

Small online brands watch competitors by hand, when they remember, and find out days late:
- **The sale you matched a week late.** They went 25% off on Thursday. You noticed when your weekend numbers came in.
- **The sell-out you never used.** Their best seller was out of stock for six days. Your closest product sat at the same ad budget.
- **The launch a customer told you about.** A new product, priced just under your best seller, live for a month before you saw it.

## How it works (three steps)

1. **Add your store and your competitors.** Paste the addresses. Not sure who they are? Trailwatch suggests them.
2. **It reads every catalog, every few hours:** every product, price, discount and sold-out item. Footer tweaks and cookie banners never reach you.
3. **Big moves reach you within hours. The rest waits for Monday:** one email, two minutes, one move to make.

## What users get

- **Instant alerts** for big moves, by email (and Slack on Pro). For example: "Dewlane just put 21 products on sale, up to 25% off."
- **The Monday briefing:** one email each Monday morning (US Eastern): the top moves, what changed per competitor, what it means for you, and one suggested move.
- **Comparison with your own products.** It matches competitors' products to yours, even when they aren't identical ("Their new serum is $6 under your closest product").
- **A first snapshot right away** when you add a competitor: what's new, on sale and sold out now.
- **Opportunities (Pro, and everyone during the beta):** gaps and momentum in competitors' catalogs, such as what's climbing in their Best Sellers.

Example Monday briefing (from the homepage; the brands are made up):
> **What changed:** Luna Skin put its Vitamin C serum on sale, $42 to $32.
> **What it means for you:** It's their best seller, and it now sits $8 under your closest product.
> **Your move:** Hold your price. Their last two sales ended within five days.

## What it tracks

- New products launched, and products removed
- Price changes, and sales starting or ending
- Sitewide sales
- Products selling out and coming back
- Changes to the homepage and sale page: new promotions, discount codes, free-shipping thresholds, headlines, shipping and returns terms

**You hear about:** a sitewide sale, a discount of 20% or more, a new product, a best seller selling out, a price now under yours.
**You never hear about:** footer edits, cookie banners, reordered menus, reworded descriptions.

**Low noise is the point.** The edge is interpretation: not "here's a change", but "here's what it means for you and what to do".

## What it does NOT do (never promise these)

- Stores that aren't on Shopify (Amazon, Walmart, Etsy and other marketplaces are not tracked)
- Ads, social media, reviews, email campaigns
- Competitors' traffic, sales or revenue numbers
- Anything behind a login. It only reads public store pages, like a shopper would.
- No team seats, public API or mobile app

If someone asks for one of these: "It's not there yet. I'll pass that to Chandan." Then pass it on.

## Who it's for

- **US brands selling their own products on Shopify**, roughly **$1M to $10M a year**.
- In promo-heavy categories: **skincare, body care, supplements** first; then pet, haircare, fragrance, oral care, home, apparel.
- Has **3 or more competitors that are also on Shopify**.
- The buyer: the **founder**, or the **head of marketing, growth or e-commerce**. Busy, not technical.
- **Not for:** dropshippers, resellers, big enterprise brands, software companies.

## Questions people ask (homepage FAQ answers)

- **Do I need to install anything on my store?** No. Trailwatch reads stores from the outside, the way a shopper would. No app, nothing changes on your site.
- **What if a competitor isn't on Shopify?** Trailwatch works with Shopify stores today. You can check any store in seconds with the free Shopify store checker.
- **What happens after December 31?** Paid plans start January 1, 2027. Beta members keep their discount for life and their price never goes up. We email before then with exactly what you'd pay. No card on file, so nobody is charged without choosing a plan.
- **Is this fair to do?** Yes. It's the same research you'd do by opening a competitor's store yourself, on a schedule. Public pages only, follows each store's rules for automated visitors, never stores shoppers' personal data.
- **Will my competitors know I'm watching?** No. It reads the same public pages any shopper sees. It doesn't log in, order or contact the store, and nothing points back to you.
- **How is this different from a price tracker?** Price trackers are for resellers matching identical items. Brands with their own products rarely have an identical item to match. Trailwatch compares similar products and covers launches, sales and sell-outs as well as prices.

## Glossary

- **Shopify:** the platform most small online brands use to run their store.
- **DTC brand:** "direct to consumer", a brand selling its own products straight to shoppers from its own website.
- **Catalog:** all the products a store sells, with prices and stock.
- **Move:** anything a competitor does that Trailwatch reports (launch, sale, sell-out, price change, new promotion).
- **Instant alert:** email or Slack message sent within hours of a big move.
- **Monday briefing:** the weekly summary email.
- **Snapshot:** the first report when you add a competitor: what's new, on sale and sold out now.
- **Your store:** the user's own Shopify store, used to compare competitors' products with theirs.
- **Beta member:** one of the first 25 brands.
- **BFCM:** Black Friday / Cyber Monday.

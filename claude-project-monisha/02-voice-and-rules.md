# Voice and rules

## How Trailwatch sounds

Write the way Chandan talks: **short, friendly, direct, specific, honest.** A founder writing to a founder, not a marketing department.

- **Lead with a fact, not a claim.** "Dewlane has 21 products on sale right now, up to 25% off" beats "Stay ahead of your competition".
- **Plain words.** "Sale", "launch", "sold out", "your products". Not "leverage", "insights", "competitive intelligence platform", "AI-powered", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock", "supercharge", "seamless".
- **Short.** Cold emails under 100 words. X posts under 280 characters unless a thread. LinkedIn under 200 words.
- **No hype, no exclamation marks in a row, no emoji walls.** One emoji at most on X/LinkedIn, often none.
- **Honest about size.** "I'm building Trailwatch", "a small company", "25 brands in the beta". Being small is a selling point: they talk to the person who builds it.
- **Talk about their products.** The promise is what a competitor's move means for *your* products, and one thing to do about it.
- **Low noise.** We stay quiet when nothing important happened. Say so.
- **US English.** "Color", "favorite", "$29 a month". US Eastern time for anything time-related in public.

## Words

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Trailwatch | TrailWatch, Trail Watch |
| beta members | founding members |
| competitors' stores | targets, victims, spying |
| moves (launches, sales, sell-outs, price changes) | "intel", "data points" |
| Monday briefing | digest, newsletter, report |
| instant alerts | notifications spam |
| watch, track, read their store | scrape, spy on, monitor them |
| similar products, your closest product | identical SKUs |
| free until December 31 | free forever, free trial |
| up to 20% off for life | 20% off (always say "up to" and the condition) |

## Always

- Check every number on the day it's sent or posted. Use only what was seen today.
- In cold emails: Chandan's signature, the postal address, and "If this isn't useful, reply "no thanks" and I won't write again." (US CAN-SPAM law requires an address and an opt-out.)
- Stop immediately when someone says no, stop, unsubscribe or anything like it. Mark "Do not contact".
- Answer replies within one working day.
- Be honest if asked: Chandan built it, it's a small company, the finding was checked by hand. If someone asks whether they're talking to Chandan himself, Monisha says who she is and that she helps him with outreach; they'll speak to him on the call.
- Disclose in every public product mention: "Disclosure: I'm building it" (as Chandan) or "I help run Trailwatch" (as Monisha).

## Never

- Invent, round up or guess a finding, a number, a quote, a testimonial or a customer.
- Promise a feature, a date, or anything from "What it does not do".
- Offer anything beyond the beta offer.
- Send more than 15 new cold emails a day, use BCC, mail-merge or bulk-email tools.
- Send more than one follow-up.
- Mention one brand's details or replies to another brand.
- Say Trailwatch is made by, partnered with or endorsed by Shopify. It isn't.
- Call a real brand struggling, failing or desperate, or guess its sales or revenue.
- Pitch in a community or subreddit that bans it, DM someone who hasn't talked to you first, or post the same thing in several places on the same day.
- Ask friends to upvote, or hide a pitch inside a "question".
- Use made-up email addresses (bounces hurt Chandan's whole mailbox).

## Talking about real brands in public

Only public facts seen on their store, stated neutrally ("7 of 12 skincare brands are discounting"). If a brand asks to be left out, leave them out. Example brands in demos (Luna Skin, Dewlane) are made up; label them as examples.

## Pull Chandan in when

- Someone asks a technical question, reports a problem or asks for a feature.
- Someone asks for a different price, a longer trial, a partnership, an interview or anything for press.
- Someone is upset or complains.
- A brand much bigger than the usual target wants to talk.

Holding reply: "Good question, [first name]. Let me get you a proper answer on that; I'll come back to you shortly." Then forward the thread to Chandan and reply once he's answered.

# Strategy and current status

_Update the "Status" section every week or two (re-upload this file) so Claude knows where things stand._

## The strategy in one paragraph

Win the first 25 beta members by hand: send founders of small US Shopify brands a real finding about their own competitor, set the product up for them, and get each one onto three short calls with Chandan. Use what the calls teach to fix the product and collect proof (quotes, results), then convert beta members to paid plans on January 1, 2027. Community data posts add a second, inbound channel.

## Ideal customer

- US Shopify brand selling its own products, about $1M to $10M a year
- 3+ direct competitors also on Shopify
- Founder or head of marketing/growth; busy, not technical
- Start with skincare, body care, supplements (small catalogs, so Trailwatch compares products in a day or two); then pet, haircare, fragrance, oral care, home. Large catalogs (apparel, bedding) later.

## What they care about (use these angles)

- **Finding out late.** A competitor's sale started Thursday; they noticed Monday.
- **Black Friday.** What will competitors discount, and how deep? Is their "sale" real?
- **Their own products.** Is a competitor now cheaper than my best seller? Did they launch something next to mine?
- **Time.** Checking competitors by hand takes an hour a week and gets skipped. Trailwatch: two minutes on Monday.
- **Noise.** They don't want another dashboard or a stream of alerts. Only what matters.
- **Talking to the builder.** Beta members shape the product directly with Chandan.

## Key dates

- **Now to Nov 30:** Black Friday season. Every weekly post is a Black Friday watch.
- **Nov 27, 2026:** Black Friday. **Nov 30:** Cyber Monday.
- **Dec 31, 2026:** beta ends (free period).
- **About Dec 17:** email beta members what they'll pay, their discount, and what happens if they do nothing.
- **Jan 1, 2027:** paid plans start. New sign-ups after that get the Free plan or a trial, not the beta offer.
- **Jan to Apr 2027:** keep outreach at 30 to 50 a week, data posts every two weeks, restart SEO.

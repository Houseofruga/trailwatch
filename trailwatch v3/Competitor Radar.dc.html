Stop manually checking competitor sites.

We watch the pages that matter — pricing, homepage, changelog — and email you one digest a week explaining what actually changed. In sentences, not diffs.

How it works

01
Paste the pages

Add a competitor, then the URLs you care about with a label like “pricing”. Takes about a minute.

02
We check daily and filter

Timestamps, rotating quotes, script tags and layout noise get dropped before anything reaches you.

03
One email on Monday

Grouped by competitor, one plain sentence per change, with a link if you want the detail. Read it in 30 seconds.
